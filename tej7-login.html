Common TEJ7 Login Problems and Fixes

Problem: "Incorrect password" error
Double-check that you are entering the password exactly as set, including correct capitalisation. If you are still getting the error, use Forgot Password to reset it.

Problem: "Number not registered" message
You may be entering a different mobile number from the one used to register. Try alternate numbers you may have used. If none work, the account may not exist — visit the TEJ7 register page to create a new account.

Problem: OTP not arriving
Wait 60 seconds and check if your phone has signal. Make sure the number you entered matches your registered number. If your inbox is full or the number has changed, contact TEJ7 support through in-app live chat.

Problem: Account temporarily suspended
Multiple failed login attempts can temporarily lock an account. Wait 15–30 minutes and try again. If the account remains locked, contact TEJ7 support via in-app chat with your registered mobile number for verification.

Problem: App crashes on login screen
Close the app completely and restart it. If it keeps crashing, clear the app's cache under Android Settings → Apps → TEJ7 → Storage → Clear Cache. If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall the latest version from the TEJ7 APK download page.

Problem: "Server error" or login loads but nothing happens
This is usually a temporary server or network issue. Check your internet connection first. If your connection is fine, wait a few minutes and try again. TEJ7 maintenance periods can occasionally cause brief login interruptions.

Tips for Keeping Your TEJ7 Account Secure

Use a strong, unique password. Avoid using the same password you use for other apps or services. A mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, and numbers is recommended.

Never share your OTP. TEJ7 support will never ask for your one-time password. Any message requesting your OTP is fraudulent — do not respond to it.

Log out on shared devices. If you access TEJ7 from a shared or borrowed phone, always log out when done under Profile → Logout.

Keep your registered number active. Your mobile number is the primary recovery method for your account. If you change your number, update it in TEJ7 before the old SIM is deactivated.
